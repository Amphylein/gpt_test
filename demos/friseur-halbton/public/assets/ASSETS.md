# Coherence assessment

Five of the eight process images are from **cottonbro studio** and come from related salon visual families. This deliberately gives the working layer a consistent photographic language.

The Max Vakhtbovych interior image is a separate context layer and is allowed to be brighter/cleaner. The Engin Akyurt finish image is used sparingly for a different service state.

The demo should never claim that all photographed interiors or people belong to one real business. They are licensed demo material for a fictional concept.
