All selected photographs below are from **Pexels** and were shown on their individual pages as `Free to use` at the time of selection.

Pexels states that photos and videos can be used free of charge, including for commercial projects, and attribution is not required. # Coherence assessment

The first four finished-project images are intentionally from the **same photographer**, which gives the demo a much more believable visual system than mixing unrelated stock images.

The workshop/process images are allowed to be rougher and darker because they represent a different narrative layer. They should not be color-graded to look artificially identical to the finished interiors; the contrast is part of the art direction.

# Repository note

The connected GitHub text-file interface used during this phase cannot directly place the binary JPEG files into the repository. The demo therefore includes download scripts under `scripts/` that fetch these exact documented assets into `public/assets/images/`.

For a finished public demo, run the asset script and serve **local files** rather than third-party hotlinks.
